# 📌 About The Project

Environmental awareness among students is often limited to theory-based learning.

**EcoApp** transforms environmental education into an interactive digital experience where students can participate in eco-friendly activities, complete challenges, earn rewards, and track their progress.

The platform connects students, teachers, and administrators through role-based access, creating a structured environment for sustainability learning.

---

# 👥 User Roles


## 🎓 Students

- Create an account
- Participate in eco challenges
- Complete environmental tasks
- Earn points and badges
- Track achievements
- Share progress


## 👩‍🏫 Teachers

- Create and manage lessons
- Add quizzes and activities
- Monitor student participation
- Verify completed activities


## 🛠️ Admin

- Manage users
- Monitor platform activities
- Verify content
- Track overall engagement
